The research

What are we solving?

Academic performance is shaped by more than study hours — nationality, engagement, attendance, and dozens of variables interplay in ways raw grades don't reveal. RAYBYTE builds a classifier pipeline over a real-world student dataset, trains multiple ML models, then surfaces the most predictive features through confusion matrices, heatmaps, and importance plots.

12+

Prediction classes — Low, Medium, High

Open-source, free-tier infrastructure

Core components

Data Pipeline
Automated ingestion with cleaning, null handling, encoding, and train/test splitting.
Multi-Model Architecture
Decision Tree, Random Forest, KNN, SVM — trained on identical splits.
Training System
Colab-based loops with hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation, and checkpoint exports.
Evaluation Suite
Precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and confusion matrices — per-class and macro-averaged.
Visual Analytics
Heatmaps, correlation matrices, feature-importance plots, and learning curves.
Inference Interface
Web UI for submitting student vectors and getting real-time grade-band predictions.
