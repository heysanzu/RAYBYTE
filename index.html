The research

What are we solving?

Academic performance is shaped by more than study hours. Nationality, engagement, attendance, hands raised — dozens of variables interplay in ways raw grades don't reveal.

RAYBYTE builds a classifier pipeline over a real-world student CSV dataset, trains multiple ML models, then surfaces the most predictive features through confusion matrices, heatmaps, and importance plots.

The result: a reproducible ML benchmark any educator or researcher can extend.

What we're building

Core components

Full-stack ML pipeline — raw CSV to interpretable predictions — covering every layer of the research stack.

Data Pipeline
Automated ingestion of the student CSV with cleaning, null handling, label encoding, and train/test splitting.
Multi-Model Architecture
Decision Tree, Random Forest, KNN, SVM and more — trained and compared on identical splits for fair benchmarking.
Training System
Colab-based training loops with hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation, and checkpoint exports for reproducibility.
Evaluation Suite
Precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and confusion matrices — metrics presented per-class and macro-averaged.
Visual Analytics
Heatmaps, correlation matrices, feature-importance plots, and learning curves — making findings legible beyond the team.
Inference Interface
A lightweight web interface for submitting student feature vectors and getting real-time grade-band predictions.
